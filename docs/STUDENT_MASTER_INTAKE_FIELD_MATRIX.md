# Secret Sharz — Student Master Intake Field Matrix

Version: 1.0  
Status: Architecture baseline  

## Purpose

This document is the implementation contract for student onboarding and profile visibility. It distinguishes information collected from the student/parent/institution/professional from information generated by the platform. It also prevents dashboard-specific forms from creating duplicate sources of truth.

## Legend

- **Student**: student may enter/edit the field subject to account and workflow rules.
- **Parent**: parent/guardian may provide or confirm the field where the workflow permits.
- **Institution**: institution may provision/confirm institutional data.
- **Professional**: assigned professional may add service-specific professional data.
- **System**: derived/generated; never ask the student to type it when it can be derived.
- **Admin**: administrative control or correction.
- **Restricted**: sensitive professional information; not part of ordinary cross-role profile views.

## Account creation rules

1. **Student account** may be created/invited by the institution, admin, or authorized professional workflow.
2. **Parent/guardian account** is activated by the parent/guardian through an invitation/link. Entering parent details does not silently create a parent account.
3. **Professional account** is created by Admin only. Public onboarding must not offer self-service Professional account creation.
4. Father, Mother and Guardian are relationship records and may map to separate accounts while using the same Parent Dashboard experience.

## Data principles

- DOB is the source of truth; age is derived.
- Assessment results are historical records; do not overwrite the assessment history with a single current score field.
- Institution entitlement, student service enrollment and professional assignment are separate concepts.
- Private clinical/professional notes are not ordinary profile fields and must remain restricted.
- Dashboards should consume the canonical student model/resolver rather than independently interpreting legacy fields.
- Legacy fields such as `parentName`, `parentUid`, `primary_path`, and `assignedCounsellorId` should be normalized for compatibility and retired only after migration validation.
